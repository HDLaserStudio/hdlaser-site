Two ways to put it on

Engraved or full color?

Not sure which? Ask, and we’ll tell you what will look best on your cup.

Laser engraving

Permanent, one tone

On a coated tumbler the laser takes off the color and shows the steel underneath, so your logo or name can’t peel or fade. Best for logos, names and fine detail.

UV printing

Full color, right on the cup

Bright, multi-color artwork printed straight onto the surface. Best for colorful logos and illustrations, like the café cups we make for Boards n’ Beans.

Questions

Custom tumbler questions

Can I bring my own tumbler?

Yes. YETI, Hydro Flask or any other brand, new or one you already love. Bring it to the shop on Turquoise St, or tell us about it in your quote request.

Engraving or full color: which should I pick?

Engraving is one tone and lasts as long as the cup, so it’s best for logos, names and fine lines. Full-color UV printing is for colorful artwork. Not sure? Ask, and we’ll tell you what will look best.

Is there a minimum order?

No. One cup is fine. For a team or company, order 6 or more and save.

What should I send for my logo?

The best file you have, like a PDF, SVG or a large PNG. A photo or a sketch works too: we redraw it for you. You see a free digital proof before anything is made.

How long does it take?

Small orders are often ready the same day. For bigger orders we give you a date with your proof. Need it by a certain day? Tell us when you ask for your quote.

Do you ship?

Yes. Pick up at the shop in Pacific Beach, or we ship it to you. Name cups can be ordered and shipped anywhere in the US right from our website.

Ready to put your name on it?

Tell us the cup, the logo or the name, and how many. We’ll email you a free quote and a digital proof.
